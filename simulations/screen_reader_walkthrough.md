# Screen Reader Simulations & Interaction Flows
Modernized from AllInPlay 'Blind Adrenaline' documentation for Napostol Games.

## 1. Interaction Philosophy
1. **Zero Mouse Requirement**: All game actions are mapped to single-key shortcuts and NumPad for blind players.
2. **Spatial Audio Panning**: Players are mapped from far-left to far-right so the sound of a card play or bet immediately identifies who acted.
3. **Audio Ducking**: When screen readers (VO, NVDA, JAWS) speak, all ambient and background sounds automatically duck by 80%.

---

## 2. Screen Reader Keyboard Mappings

- **Hand Inspection**: `H` speaks your entire hand grouped by suit.
- **Community Cards**: `C` speaks the Flop, Turn, and River.
- **Current Trick / Table**: `T` speaks the current trick or active bets.
- **Pot / Score**: `P` speaks total pot or current scores and bags.
- Actions: `C` to Check/Call, `F` to Fold, `R` to Raise, `Space` to Play Selected Card.
